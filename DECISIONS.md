# Decisions

Gap decisions and judgement calls made while building the prototype. Precedence used throughout:
hard constraints > architecture.md > product.md > design.md > the build brief (the brief wins where it is more specific).
All four source documents were present and were not edited.

| # | Decision | Why | Related |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Routing:** `HashRouter` with `#/` (S1), `#/welcome`, `#/subscription/new` and `#/subscription/:id` (S2), `#/offers` (S3), `#/settings` (S4); anything else redirects to `#/`. S1.1 import, S2.1 letter, offer details and the budget sheet are overlays held in component state, not routes. Vite `base: './'`. | Works on GitHub Pages and from any sub-path without server rewrites; overlays don't need deep links. | architecture §3 (routes), product §4.1 |
| 2 | **First launch:** a persisted `onboarded` flag (default `false`). Until it is set, every route redirects to Welcome ("See every subscription in one place"). "Explore the demo" loads `seed.json`; "Add manually" starts empty and opens S2; "Import bank statement" starts empty and opens S1 with S1.1. After an empty start, S1 shows a dismissible "Set a monthly budget?" card once. Settings › Demo tools has "Reset demo data" (reseed, stay onboarded) and a quieter "Start over" (clear everything, back to Welcome). | product Flow 1 asks for a Welcome screen and a skippable budget prompt; this replaces architecture §7's silent seeding on first launch. | product §3.2, §3.4 Flow 1; architecture §7 |
| 3 | **Model additions:** `Subscription.cancelledAt?` and `notes?`; persisted `onboarded` and `budgetPromptDismissed`. "Saved per month" = monthly equivalents of subscriptions with status `cancelled` and a `cancelledAt`; the SummaryCard shows "Saving €X/month from cancellations" only when it is above 0. Cancelled subscriptions stay listed (muted, "Cancelled" badge) but are excluded from totals, alerts and offers. | The € saved metric (product §1.4) needs to know what was cancelled through the app. | architecture §4; product §1.4 |
| 4 | **Totals and shared plans:** totals always use the full price; the user's share appears only on the card badge ("Shared 2/3 paid") and in S2. Paid = members with `paid: true` / all members; the user is always the first member and always counts as paid. | One consistent meaning of "what you pay for"; the split is about who owes whom. | product Flow 7 |
| 5 | **Trials:** a trial counts as €0 while today < `trialEndDate`, then at `priceAfterTrial`. The stored status is never mutated; `effectiveStatus(sub, today)` returns `active` after the end date. Offers compare trials against `priceAfterTrial`. | Stays correct when the demo date moves backwards and forwards. | architecture §5 |
| 6 | **Statement parsing:** rows with amount < 0 and amount = 0 are kept (the €0 case matters for trials). `parseAmount` handles `-13,99`, `-13.99`, `-1.234,56`, `-1,234.56`; `parseDate` handles `2026-08-12`, `12.08.2026`, `12/08/2026` (day first). Columns are matched case-insensitively (`date/buchungstag/datum`, `description/verwendungszweck/beschreibung/empfänger`, `amount/betrag`). Merchants are normalised (lowercase; `*`, `#`, digits, dates, `ref`, `sepa`, `lastschrift`, `kartenzahlung` and extra spaces removed). Grouping key: a catalogue service whose space-less name appears in the space-less text (so `MOVIEMAX TRIAL VERIFY` and `MOVIEMAX SUBSCRIPTION` join), else the first two words. Display name: catalogue name, else the key in Title Case. Recurring only if every interval is 28–33 or 360–370 days and every amount is within ±5% of the median. | Matches the sample statement exactly: 6 recurring + 1 trial, no noise. | architecture §5.1 |
| 7 | **i18n and formatting:** every UI string goes through `t(key, vars)` with `{var}` interpolation and `_one`/`_other` plurals chosen by `count`; `en` and `de` have identical key sets (tested). Money via `Intl.NumberFormat('en-IE')` → "€11.99" and `('de-DE')` → "11,99 €". Dates are DD.MM.YYYY in both languages; within 7 days they are relative ("today", "tomorrow", "in 3 days" / "heute", "morgen", "in 3 Tagen"). | product §2.4, §4.3. | architecture §2 (Translations) |
| 8 | **Budget:** set from the S1 BudgetBar ("Set a budget" link, or tap the bar → "Monthly budget" sheet with Save/Remove) and from Settings › Reminders. A limit of 0 or none means no budget: the bar shows the NoBudget state and there is no budget alert. | — | design §5.2 BudgetBar |
| 9 | **Family filter:** the S1 chip "Everyone ▾" opens a menu with Everyone plus each member. Unassigned subscriptions belong to the first member, "Me". The filter changes the list, SummaryCard, BudgetBar and category chart; alerts and tips always cover everyone. | Alerts are about money leaving the household, whoever it is for. | product §4.2 S1, user story 9 |
| 10 | **Alerts:** sorted by type rank, then due date: cancel_deadline 1, trial_end 1, price_change 2, yearly_renewal 3, renewal 3, budget 4 (stored in `Alert.urgency`). The AlertBox shows the top 3; "Show all (n)" expands in place and "Show less" collapses. An alert with a subscription opens S2; the budget alert opens the budget sheet. One alert per type per subscription, and a cancel deadline suppresses the renewal alert of the same subscription. | product §4.3: deadlines and trial ends first. | architecture §5 |
| 11 | **Countdown badge:** (1) trial not yet ended → "Trial ends"; (2) notice period > 0 and the last day to cancel within 30 days → "Cancel by"; (3) otherwise "Renews" with the next renewal. Cancelled subscriptions show none. | Shows the date that actually needs action. | design §5.2 CountdownBadge |
| 12 | **Offers:** at most one offer per subscription (the biggest saving); "Save up to €X/month" is the sum of those. The BottomNav badge = matched offers + duplicate groups, hidden at 0. "View" opens a sheet with title, description, price, saving, "Replaces {name}", the Sponsored badge, [Switch] (opens S2 of the replaced subscription with `?cancel=1`) and [Close]. | Seed result: 7 offers, €50.00/month, one duplicate group, badge 8 (tested). | product Flow 8; architecture §5.3 |
| 13 | **Toast:** one at a time, hidden after 3 s, `role="status"` with `aria-live="polite"`. Used for Added, Saved, "Marked as cancelled. You'll save €X/month." (with Undo), Copied, import success, "Demo data reset", plus budget saved/removed, deleted and reactivated. | No toast library allowed. | design §7 |
| 14 | **Print:** the letter's Print action calls `window.print()`; `@media print` in `index.css` prints only `[data-print-root]`, black on white, 12 pt, A4 with 20 mm margins. The print copy is a body-level element rendered next to the preview, and everything else is `display: none` in print, so the bottom sheet's scroll area can't clip the letter and the hidden page can't add blank pages (verified: one A4 page). | — | architecture §5.2; design §5.2 LetterPreview |
| 15 | **Branch:** work happened on `claude/zealous-curie-yegj12` instead of `feature/app`. | The cloud session that built this may only push to its assigned branch. | brief §13 |
| 16 | **Colour tokens adjusted for AA:** light `success` `#1E8E5A` → `#177347` (was 4.14:1 on white), light `warning` `#B26A00` → `#8A5200` (was 4.24:1 on white, 3.81:1 on `warning-soft`), dark `primary-soft` `#2C2550` → `#251F45` (dark `primary` on it was 4.33:1). Every other value is as specified. | design §3.1 requires all text/background pairs to pass AA; the rule wins over the three values that fail it. Storybook's a11y checks now pass in both themes. | design §3.1, §8 |
| 17 | **Category tile colours:** design.md gives no colours for news, gaming and food; they reuse music, streaming and sports hues. Dark tiles use a saturated version of each hue at 20% opacity, because the pastel light value at 20% is invisible on the dark surface. This map in `lib/categories.ts` is the only place with literal colours. | design §3.1 says "same hue at 20% opacity over surface". | design §3.1 |
| 18 | **Extra tokens:** `paper` and `ink` (the letter is white paper in both themes) plus `slide-up`/`fade-in` keyframes, on top of design §4's config and the brief's `sponsored`, `space-1…8`, `standard` easing, `fast/base/slow` durations and `radius-full`. | design §5.2 LetterPreview: "also white in dark mode". | design §4 |
| 19 | **`Service.billingCycle`:** catalogue entries have a cycle so PixelEdit pre-fills as yearly. | architecture §4 `Service` has no cycle, but S2 should pre-fill it. | architecture §4 |
| 20 | **Last day to cancel never lies in the past:** without a reachable minimum term, it uses the first renewal R with R − notice ≥ today and returns R − notice (KickPass on 20.11.2026: R = 15.01.2027 → 16.12.2026). The termination date is the minimum term end if today + notice still reaches it, else the first renewal on or after today + notice (KickPass 15.01.2027; FitZone 28.02.2027). | architecture §5 would give 15.11.2026 for KickPass, which has already passed. | architecture §5 |
| 21 | **Countdown text** follows the 7-day relative rule: "in 3 days" within a week, otherwise the date ("Renews 12.12.2026"). | design §5.2's example "in 12 days" conflicts with product §4.3 and the brief. | product §4.3 |
| 22 | **Small buttons are 44 px tall** (not 40 px). | design §8 requires touch targets of at least 44 × 44 px; that beats §3.4's 40 px. | design §3.4, §8 |
| 23 | **Badge icons are 16 px** (inline icons elsewhere are 20 px, nav/FAB 24 px, all stroke 1.75). | A 20 px icon dwarfs the 13 px badge text. | design §3.6 |
| 24 | **S2 sections:** each optional section has a switch. Off means "doesn't apply" and its values aren't saved (trial off = not a trial; contract off = no notice period, minimum term or provider details). Sections open by themselves when they hold data; picking a catalogue service turns Contract terms on. Paid ticks on an existing split are saved immediately, like a checklist; everything else waits for Save. | Toggles are the specified pattern; without immediate saving, "tick Jonas → back" in the walkthrough would lose the tick. | product §3.4 Flows 2, 7 |
| 25 | **S2 form logic lives in `lib/subscriptionForm.ts`** (pre-fill, validation, conversion, split shares), so it is tested. Price fields are text inputs with `inputMode="decimal"` and accept a comma or a dot; German shows "11,99". | No business logic in components; German users type commas. | architecture §10.7 |
| 26 | **Oversized files (> 2 MB) get the same CSV error copy** as unreadable ones; the hint under "Choose CSV file" states the 2 MB limit. | Brief: ">2 MB or no recognisable columns → CSV error copy". | design §7 |
| 27 | **The only network request** is "Use sample statement", which fetches `./sample-statement.csv` from the app's own origin. Tests swap `statementSource` for fixture text. Chosen files are read with `FileReader`; their text and transactions only exist inside one function call and are discarded, and only confirmed subscriptions are stored. | Hard constraints 1 and 3; product O1. | architecture §6 |
| 28 | **The letter uses the form as it stands** when the form is valid (so a customer number typed a moment ago appears), otherwise the saved data. | What you see is what you send. | product Flow 4 |
| 29 | **The card's price-change tag** shows a change from the last 30 days, the same window as the price alert. | Older changes are visible in the S2 price history. | design §5.2 SubscriptionCard |
| 30 | **Download name** keeps the service name's spelling: `cancellation-FitZone-2026-11-20.txt` / `Kündigung-FitZone-2026-11-20.txt`. Characters other than letters, digits and umlauts become `-`; the date is ISO so files sort. | Brief: `Kündigung-{name}-{date}.txt`. | brief §11 S2.1 |
| 31 | **Recharts is lazy-loaded** with the S2 price-history chart, which halves the first bundle (365 kB instead of 743 kB minified). | Lighthouse performance ≥ 90 and Vite's chunk-size warning. | architecture §8 |
| 32 | **Onboarding guard:** only not-yet-onboarded users are redirected (to Welcome). Welcome checks once on mount and sends already-onboarded users to S1, so a stray `#/welcome` can't wipe data. | With React Router's `v7_startTransition`, a guard reacting to `onboarded` mid-click raced the Welcome buttons' own navigation. | brief §3 First launch |
| 33 | **React Router v7 future flags** (`v7_startTransition`, `v7_relativeSplatPath`) are on in the app, the tests and Storybook. | Removes the v6 deprecation warnings from the dev console. | architecture §8 (no console errors) |
| 34 | **Date inputs are native `<input type="date">`**, so the browser shows them in its own locale format (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY in US Chrome). Every date the app itself renders is DD.MM.YYYY. | No date-picker library allowed. | brief §2 |
| 35 | **"Me" is shown as "Ich" in German.** The member keeps the id `me` and the stored name "Me". | Full German UI without changing data. | brief §3 Family filter |
| 36 | **Reset demo data keeps the chosen language and theme.** | They are preferences, not demo data; switching to English mid-presentation would be jarring. | brief §3 First launch |
| 37 | **Destructive actions confirm first:** Mark as cancelled, Delete, Reset demo data and Start over each open a confirmation sheet whose safe option says "Keep it" / "Go back". Undo after cancelling restores the previous status (trial or active). | design §1.3: no dark patterns, and nothing that loses data by accident. | design §1 |
| 38 | **Copy not given in design §7** (renewal, yearly renewal, over-budget, most-expensive tip, form errors) was written in the same voice: sentence case, no exclamation marks, verbs first on buttons. | — | design §7 |
| 39 | **S3 states that sponsored offers are paid placements** and never appear in alerts or the cancellation flow. No ad banner was built. | product §1.3 promises clear labelling; the banner is a business-model idea outside F8's scope for this prototype. | product §1.3, F8 |
| 40 | **Already-tracked detection** matches by catalogue id, or by the subscription's space-less name equalling or containing the grouping key (or the other way round), names of 3+ characters only. | Catches StreamFlix and TuneBox in the seed without false positives in the noise rows. | architecture §5.1 step 5 |
| 41 | **Overlays:** focus goes to a chosen field (e.g. the budget input) or the first focusable element. A scrolling sheet body becomes focusable only when it actually overflows. | axe `scrollable-region-focusable` for long sheets without extra tab stops in short ones. | design §8 |
| 42 | **No ESLint.** It is optional in the brief; strict TypeScript, tests and the axe checks cover correctness and accessibility. | Fewer dependencies. | brief §2 |
| 43 | **Quality checks were automated headlessly:** every Storybook story (132, light and dark) passes axe-core with the addon's default rules, and so do the app's screens and sheets in light/dark × EN/DE (48 checks, 0 violations). The walkthrough, a 195 px viewport (390 px at 200% browser zoom) and 200% text size were checked in Chromium with Playwright: no horizontal scroll, no console errors, no requests to other origins. Lighthouse 12 (mobile) on `npm run preview` scored performance/accessibility/best practices 99/100/100 for Welcome and 100/100/100 for the seeded dashboard. | The cloud build environment has no browser UI; a hands-on pass on real devices is still worth doing. | design §8; architecture §8 |
